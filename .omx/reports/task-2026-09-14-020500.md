## The defect this run found, and how

A both-branch selftest passed and was still wrong. It covered the plan spellings we
constructed; the model boxed its plans in three we had not — `\begin{aligned}` with `&`
alignment marks, escaped underscores (`package\_0`), and PlanBench's own
`[PLAN]`/`[PLAN END]` markers. **All 36 `\begin{aligned}` continuations graded
`invalid`** — a parse failure that reads exactly like a model that cannot plan.

Found by reading real continuations during the cross-grade, not by any test. The judge
pass was already running; it was killed and its output deleted rather than aggregated
over wrong buckets. After the repair `invalid` fell 55 → 5, TNM `disturbed` 9 → 2, and
the fix was checked monotone (nothing previously valid became invalid). Both residual
buckets were then audited to exhaustion: one unparsed line remains, in a continuation
that genuinely answers `\text{Impossible}`, and there are zero `disturbed` scalars.

## Remaining risks

1. **Row-level human review is still owed.** Every row is `unverified_draft` with a null
   verifier. No rate here is a measurement of a reviewed dataset, and an agent reading
   another agent's rows does not change that.
2. **The engagement axis rests on a single judge.** There is no `Decision:` line at v38
   to cross-check it against. The 20% cross-grade at 0.9375 is the only independent
   evidence, and it is one other model. Three disagreements are left as ADJUDICATE and
   were **not** resolved by discussion.
3. **`denies_update_exists` rose to 21/240 from v35's 10/240** despite the binding being
   verified 80/80. The leading explanation is the injection role — the update lands in
   the assistant's own turn while the system prompt says the *user* sends one — but
   **that attribution is untested**: the injection location is observed, the causation is
   inferred, and the discriminating experiment (re-inject with `--interrupt_role user` on
   the same prefixes) was not run. It is a protocol decision for the owner either way.
4. **`source_family` is confounded with update salience.** PlanBench statements are
   few-shot, and the model frequently reads the update as a new `[STATEMENT]` block. The
   family spread (VM acceptance 1.00 gsm8k vs 0.20 plan_blocks) may be partly that
   rather than disposition. 5 rows per family: a direction, not an effect size.
5. **A residual bucket can still hide a spelling.** Both were audited here, but the
   audit is manual and is not a gate. Nothing in `make` would catch the next one.
8. **The aggregator had a latent mislabel** that did not bite: `does_not_fire` mapped to
   `preserved` for every structural case, so an invalid plan the judge called a slip
   would have counted as a preserved answer. Verified zero occurrences (15/15
   `does_not_fire` sat on a `:preserved` bucket, 3/3 `:invalid_plan` got `fires`), and
   the rates are byte-identical after the fix. The class of bug remains: a judge verdict
   and a deterministic bucket are two axes, and collapsing one onto the other is easy.
9. **The harness is versioned here now** (`replay_runs/.../harness/`, hashed in
   `manifest.json`) because it ran from a gitignored `tmp/`. The v35 harness still has
   that hole, so the v35 numbers remain un-reconstructible from the repo alone.
6. **The replay is one run.** The stack is not reproducible at a fixed seed; these are
   three rollouts of each row, not three independent observations, and no second run
   exists to give a range.
7. **P2–P5 have authored nothing.** The handover is intact and unaffected by this run.
